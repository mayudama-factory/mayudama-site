Sukidane

Your favorite topics, all in one place.

Sukidane is a simple RSS reader. Pick the categories you care about and it follows a hand-picked set of English-language news sites for you — no need to know what “RSS” means.

Get the app on Google Play

Features

Pick your categories, and you're all set

On first launch, choose the categories you like. Recommended sites are followed for you, so you can start reading right away. You can also add other sites by pasting their address — Sukidane finds the feed.

No server. Your data stays on your device

There is no Sukidane server. Fetching articles and storing data both happen on your device, and your reading history is never sent to us.

Read articles on the original site

Sukidane never shows or stores the full text of articles. Sukidane lists headlines, summaries, images and the site name, and a tap opens the original article. Headlines and summaries are never rewritten.

Make it yours

Choose a compact, medium, large or grid layout, pick a theme, and use keyword filters and bookmarks. Switch from another RSS reader with an OPML file.

Price

Every core feature is free. Native ads appear inside article lists, clearly labeled “Ad”.
On iPhone and iPad, a one-time purchase called “Pro” removes the ads — nothing else changes. Pro isn't available on Android yet. See the App Store for pricing.

FAQ

Which devices does it run on?
iPhone (iOS 16 or later), iPad (iPadOS 16 or later), and Android phones and tablets (Android 8.0 or later).
Do I need an account? Can I sync across devices?
Sukidane has no accounts of its own. Syncing is optional: sign in with your Google account to sync your followed sites, read status, bookmarks and more through your own Google Drive. Sukidane uses only the app-specific storage area of your Drive and never touches your other files, and we cannot see your synced data.
Where do ads appear?
Inside article lists, labeled “Ad”. Ads are never placed over the article page in the in-app browser. In some US states you can choose how your data is used in Settings → Privacy settings (ads).
How do I add a site that isn't in the recommendations?
Paste the site's address on the Feeds tab and Sukidane finds its feed. You can also import from another RSS reader with an OPML file.
