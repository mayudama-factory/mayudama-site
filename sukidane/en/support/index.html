Frequently asked questions

Where is my data kept?
Sukidane has no server of its own. Feeds are fetched directly from your device, and what you save stays on your device. If you turn on sync, your settings are stored in your own Google Drive (app data area only). See the Privacy Policy.

New articles are slow to appear (iPhone).
Automatic refresh runs at most once per hour per feed. On iPhone it also depends on iOS: check that Settings → General → Background App Refresh is on for Sukidane and that Low Power Mode is off. You can always pull down on a list to refresh.

How do I change the language?
Sukidane follows your device language (Japanese or English). To change it, open Settings → Language. During first-time setup you can switch between Japanese sites and English sites at any time, and add the other later.

How do I restore a purchase?
Open Settings, go to the Pro section and tap “Restore purchase”. It works with the same Apple ID or Google account that made the purchase.

How do I add a site that is not in the list?
Add it by its feed (RSS/Atom) URL from the Feeds tab. Sites you add yourself are your own choice, as in any RSS reader.
