Frequently asked questions

The Wi-Fi name (SSID) is not shown.
iOS requires the Location permission (While Using) to read the SSID. Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services and set Network Field Kit to “While Using the App”. The app does not record or send your location. The SSID is not shown on a cellular connection, while a VPN is active, or when you are not on Wi-Fi.

The app asks for the Local Network permission.
It asks only when you open a feature that talks to devices on the same network, such as LAN discovery. LAN discovery and the ARP screen send name queries (mDNS and NetBIOS) to devices on the same LAN. Other features work without it. You can change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.

Why can’t the app show a MAC address?
iOS does not give apps the MAC address of your own device or information about nearby access points. The app does not show information as if it were available when it is not. For other devices on your LAN, it shows what iOS provides.

Where does the speed test connect to?
The speed test runs against Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com), which receives your device’s IP address and the test traffic. A consent dialog is shown before the first test, and the test starts only after you tap “Agree and start”. You can review and withdraw your consent in the app’s Settings under “Speed test consent”. A test uses a lot of data on cellular. See the Privacy Policy.

About ads.
The free version shows one banner ad at the bottom of the screen. Buying Pro removes it. All features work even if you do not allow tracking (ATT) or the consent prompt (UMP); in that case ads are not personalized. Ads are personalized only for users whose App Store country or region is Japan, the United States, the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland; in other countries and regions the app does not ask for tracking permission and ads are never personalized. You can change permissions in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and in the app’s Settings under “Privacy settings (ads)” (shown depending on your region).

What does Pro include?
Every diagnostic tool is free and unlimited. Pro (one-time purchase) adds: no ads, CSV / JSON export of results, PDF export, sharing and printing for diagnostic reports and test plans, unlimited history with search, unlimited bookmarks and saved test plans with iCloud sync, saved device names, and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?
In the app’s Settings, open “View Pro, purchase or restore” and tap “Restore Purchases”. It works with the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

About iCloud sync.
Sync is a Pro feature and is off by default. When on, history (which may include SSIDs), bookmarks, report drafts, test plans and names you give LAN devices are saved to your own iCloud (settings such as thresholds are not synced) and shared with your other devices signed in with the same Apple ID. Your device must be signed in to iCloud, and iCloud must be allowed for this app. If it does not sync, check Settings > your name > iCloud.

The widget does not update or shows no IP address.
Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets are a Pro feature. Widgets do not communicate. They show the latest public IP address and connection state the app obtained, so open the app and let the Home screen load first. On the Lock Screen the display is hidden while the device is locked.

Can I use it from Shortcuts?
Yes. You can run diagnostics such as Ping and DNS as Shortcuts actions. They do not open the app, and the result is returned as text for later actions in your shortcut.

Known limitations

  • Because of iOS, the MAC address of your own device and information about nearby access points are not available.
  • Depending on the iOS version, the MAC addresses of other devices on your LAN may not be available.
  • Some network equipment is set not to answer Ping and similar probes. No answer does not always mean a fault.
  • Speed test results vary with the test server, time of day, congestion and device state. Use them as a guide.
  • SNMP supports v2c only. The community string is sent in clear text.
  • With a VPN or certain network settings, results may differ from usual.
